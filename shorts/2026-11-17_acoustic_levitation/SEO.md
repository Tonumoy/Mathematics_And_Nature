Can Sound Lift Water? 🔊💧 Acoustic Levitation #shorts

## Description
Sound is holding this water. No strings, no magnets: just 72 tiny speakers singing at 40,000 Hz, too high for you to hear.

What you see: our own 3D render of a single-axis acoustic levitator (TinyLev design). Waves from the top and bottom crash into each other and make a standing wave. Where they cancel, the pressure stops shaking: a pressure node. The drop gets pushed into that quiet pocket and stays. There is one every half wavelength:
λ/2 = c / 2f = 343 / 80,000 m ≈ 4.3 mm
Turn the loudness down and the drop falls (shown in slow motion). Turn it up and it flattens into a disc, then bursts. In between, it floats. Chemists use this to dry drug droplets with nothing touching them.
