TITLE: What if the Sun became a Black Hole? 🌍🕳️ Einstein's real maths #shorts

DESCRIPTION:
What if the Sun turned into a black hole with the same mass? Would Earth get sucked in?
Nope. Same mass = same pull. From 150 million km away, Earth keeps the same orbit and the same 365-day year. It just gets very cold and dark.
(The real Sun can't do this: it's too light, and it will end as a white dwarf. This is a "what if".)

So what does a black hole actually eat? Light that comes too close.
We solve the real path of light in Einstein's gravity (Schwarzschild metric, RK45 solver):
u'' + u = 3Mu²   (u = 1/distance, M = mass)
• aim 5.30 M → light whips around and escapes
• aim 5.15 M → trapped forever
• the exact edge is 3√3 M = 5.196 M: that's the black hole's "shadow"
• for a Sun-mass black hole the shadow is about 15 km wide (the event horizon is about 6 km)
In 2019 the Event Horizon Telescope, a planet-sized network of radio dishes, photographed the shadow of M87*. Its size matched Einstein's prediction.
