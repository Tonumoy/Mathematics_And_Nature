# Facts on screen (checked 23 Nov 2026)
- 1859: Riemann's paper on counting primes; 167 years to 2026.
- ζ(s) = Σ 1/n^s (Re s > 1); zeros and |ζ| values computed with mpmath.
- Zeros verified numerically to height 3·10¹² (~12 trillion zeros), Platt & Trudgian 2021.
- Explicit formula (von Mangoldt 1895): ψ(x) = x − Σρ x^ρ/ρ − log 2π − ½ log(1 − x⁻²); the Short sums the first 600 zero pairs.
- 20 Nov 2026: Levent Alpöge (Anthropic), with Claude, released a Lean proof (~17M lines, 175,187 modules).
- 21 Nov 2026: joint statement of the Lean and Mathlib communities (endorsed by the Lean FRO and the Mathlib Initiative) certifying the statement and the proof.
- Mathlib's statement: ∀ s : ℂ, riemannZeta s = 0 → (¬∃ n : ℕ, s = -2 * (n + 1)) → s ≠ 1 → s.re = 1/2.
- Still open: human-readable paper, expert review; Clay prize needs publication + 2 years.
