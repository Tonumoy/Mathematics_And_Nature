L01 Every time you open your bank app, two secret numbers guard your money.
L02 Two thousand years ago, Caesar hid his messages by shifting every letter three steps. A became D.
L03 Easy to crack. Some letters show up far more than others. Count them, and the secret falls out.
L04 So people switched to long secret keys. New problem: how do you send the key without a thief grabbing it?
L05 In 1977, three scientists found the answer: a padlock anyone can snap shut, but only you can open.
L06 The trick? Multiplying two big prime numbers is easy. Going backwards is brutally hard.
L07 Today's lock number is six hundred seventeen digits long. Nobody has ever cracked one.
L08 But in 2025, a Google researcher estimated a big enough quantum computer could crack it in under a week. That machine doesn't exist. Yet.
L09 So in 2024, the US approved new locks, built on a puzzle where even quantum computers have no known shortcut.
L10 Every fix created a new problem. Will your bank change its locks in time?
